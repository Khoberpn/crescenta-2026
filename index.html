About CRESCENTA

CRESCENTA is an annual science and technology competition that encourages students to innovate, collaborate, think critically, and solve real-world problems through science, robotics, engineering, programming, mathematics, and technology.

Organized by the Boarding School Student Organization (OPBS) of MAN 1 Surakarta, CRESCENTA 2026 aims to empower scientific innovation and technology as catalysts for transformative civilization.

Targeting Junior High School and Senior High School students across Java and Bali, the competition features seven exciting categories designed to challenge and inspire the next generation of innovators.

Competition Categories

Mathematics Olympiad

Sharpen your reasoning skills and solve complex mathematical problems

Mathematics Olympiad

An individual competition designed to enhance your talent, understanding, and logical reasoning in mathematics. The competition consists of highly competitive preliminary and final rounds

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Individual competition format
  • Preliminary and final rounds
  • Compete for the top 5 champion titles

Science Olympiad

Explore your intellectual capabilities in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics

Science Olympiad

An individual competition aimed at expanding intellectual knowledge and analytical skills in Natural Sciences. It serves as an effective platform to motivate students in exploring the wonders of science.

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Covers Biology, Chemistry, & Physics
  • Preliminary and final rounds
  • Compete for the top 5 champion titles

Lomba Cerdas Cermat (LCC)

Test your team's intelligence, accuracy, and quick-thinking skills

Lomba Cerdas Cermat (LCC)

A fast-paced, team-based competition testing knowledge across Math, Science, English, and General Knowledge. It features a written preliminary round and an exciting buzzer-based final round.

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Team-based multidisciplinary trivia
  • Written test & buzzer-based finals
  • Compete for the top 5 champion titles

Robosoccer

Build, strategize, and pilot your robot in a thrilling soccer match.

Robosoccer

A robotics competition where custom-designed robots compete in 1-on-1 soccer matches. Featuring a Single Elimination bracket, teams must work together using one pilot and one strategist.

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Wireless manual robot control
  • Single elimination bracket system
  • Compete for the top 3 champion titles

Robot Maze Solving

Program agile robots to navigate and conquer complex tracks

Robot Maze Solving

A team-based competition designed to test programming skills and robotic agility. Teams must use strategic thinking to complete the designated track in the fastest time to achieve the highest score.

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Team-based programming challenge
  • Fastest time & highest score wins
  • Compete for the top 4 champion titles

Innovation Project

Create innovative microcontroller solutions for real-world problems

Innovation Project

Develop robotics and IoT solutions using microcontrollers like Arduino, Wemos, or ESP to solve everyday challenges. Participants will build their prototypes and pitch their mechanisms to the judges.

  • Junior High School (SMP/MTs) level
  • Build with Arduino, Wemos, or ESP
  • Prototype assembly & live pitc
  • Compete for the top 5 champion titles

Scientific Essay

Showcase your critical thinking and innovative solutions through writing.

Scientific Essay

A competition for high school students to express analytical ideas and arguments regarding current issues. Finalists will have the opportunity to present and defend their essays in front of expert judges.

  • High School (SMA/MA) level
  • Essay writing & live presentation
  • Compete for the top 5 champion titles

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in CRESCENTA?

+

CRESCENTA is open to Junior High School and Senior High School students across Java and Bali.

How do I register for the competition?

+

Registration is done online through our website. Visit the Registration page and fill out the form.

What are the competition categories?

+

CRESCENTA features seven categories: Mathematics Olympiad, Science Olympiad, Quiz Bowl (LCC), Robosoccer, Robot Maze Solving, Innovation Project, and Scientific Essay.

Is there a registration fee?

+

Yes, there is a modest registration fee to cover operational costs. Details are available on the Registration page.

What prizes are available?

+

Winners receive trophies, medals, certificates, and exciting tech prizes. All participants get certificates of participation.

Where will the competition be held?

+

The competition will be held at MAN 1 Surakarta and surrounding venues for specific categories.
